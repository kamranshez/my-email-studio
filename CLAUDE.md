# Email Studio

This studio is where we plan and write email campaigns for a small business.
We draft ideas, shape them into campaigns, and keep everything organised in
the folders below.

## Folders

- **brand/** — the business's voice, style, colours, and anything that keeps our emails on-brand.
- **leads/** — information about the people and groups we want to reach (sample or anonymised data only).
- **campaigns/** — one place for each campaign: plans, drafts, and final email copy.
- **notes/** — loose ideas, research, and working notes.

## Rules

1. **Write in plain English.** Short sentences, everyday words, no jargon.
2. **Never send email to real people.** We only plan and draft here. Do not send, schedule, or forward any email.
3. **Ask me before deleting anything.** Always check with me first, even for a single file.

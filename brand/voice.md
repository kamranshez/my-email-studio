# VCAgents Brand Voice

**Who we are:** VCAgents makes AI voice agents for call center marketing and support. They plug into open-source phone systems: VICIdial, Asterisk, FreeSWITCH, OpenSIPS and Kamailio.

**Who we write to:** Call center owners, and VoIP or SIP trunk providers who run dialers and PBX services. They know telephony well. They want facts, not hype.

## 1. Our voice in three words

**Technical. Expert. Reliable.**

- **Technical:** Name the real systems and real numbers. Say "works with VICIdial", not "works with your tools".
- **Expert:** Write like someone who has run a dialer. Be calm and sure. Don't over-explain the basics to people who already know them.
- **Reliable:** Promise only what we can deliver. Plain facts build more trust than big words.

## 2. Words we use

- drop-in
- works with VICIdial (and Asterisk, FreeSWITCH, OpenSIPS, Kamailio)
- concurrent calls
- SIP trunk
- 24/7

Industry terms like these are fine here. Our readers use them every day. Keep the sentences around them short and plain.

## 3. Words we avoid

| Avoid | Why | Try instead |
|---|---|---|
| revolutionary | Empty hype | Say what it actually does |
| game-changer | Empty hype | Give a real result or number |
| robot | Sounds cheap and cold | AI voice agent |
| replace your agents | Scares owners and their staff | handles overflow, covers after-hours, works alongside your team |
| act now | Pushy, salesy | A clear, calm next step, like "Book a demo" |

## 4. Claims we must never make

- **"100% uptime."** No system can promise this. We can describe how we keep calls running.
- **"Guaranteed sales increase."** Results depend on the customer's lists, scripts and offer.
- **"Callers can't tell it's AI."** This is misleading and may break disclosure rules. Never suggest hiding that callers are talking to AI.

If you're not sure a claim is true and provable, leave it out.

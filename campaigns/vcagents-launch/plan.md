# VCAgents Launch: Campaign Plan

**Status:** Draft plan. Nothing here is sent or scheduled.
**Dates:** Mon 7 Dec 2026 to Sun 3 Jan 2027 (4 weeks)
**Channel:** Email only. No paid ads.

---

## 1. Campaign overview

**Name:** "Live on Your Dialer"

**Summary:** Invite our 2,000 waitlist members to book a demo of VCAgents, our AI voice agent that works with VICIdial, Asterisk, FreeSWITCH, OpenSIPS and Kamailio.

**Main goal:** 300 waitlist members book a demo by Sun 3 Jan 2027.

That is 15% of the list. It's a high target, even for a warm waitlist. We should treat it as a stretch and watch the numbers from week 1 (see Risks).

**Other goals:**
- Learn which of the three groups books most, so we know where to aim next.
- Collect real setup questions (dialer, trunk, call volume) to shape future emails.

---

## 2. Audience

About 2,000 people who joined the waitlist. They already know what we do, so this is the decision stage, not awareness.

| Group | Who they are | What they care about | What worries them |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Call center owners** | Run their own floor, often on VICIdial | Covering after-hours and overflow without hiring more staff | Setup time, call quality, upsetting their agents |
| **VoIP / SIP trunk resellers** | Sell trunks and hosted PBX to call centers | A new service to resell on top of their SIP trunks | Whether it fits their stack (Asterisk, FreeSWITCH, OpenSIPS, Kamailio) and their margins |
| **BPO managers** | Run campaigns for several clients | Handling call spikes and 24/7 cover across client accounts | Client approval, disclosure rules, reporting |

**Short profile:** A call center owner or BPO manager running VICIdial who loses calls at peak times and after hours. They want more calls answered without hiring more staff. They trust facts, specs and a live demo over sales talk.

**Before launch:** We need to tag each waitlist contact by group. If the waitlist form didn't ask, send everyone the shared emails and use clicks in Email 1 to sort them into groups.

## 3. Key messages

**Core message:** VCAgents is a drop-in AI voice agent for your dialer. It handles overflow and after-hours calls and works alongside your team.

**Supporting messages:**

1. **It works with what you already run.** Drop-in for VICIdial, Asterisk, FreeSWITCH, OpenSIPS and Kamailio. No new phone system.
   *Proof needed:* setup steps and typical setup time for each platform.
2. **It covers the gaps, not your team.** It handles overflow, covers after-hours and answers 24/7, so your agents can focus on live calls.
   *Proof needed:* one pilot or test result (calls handled, missed calls reduced).
3. **It scales with your call volume.** Set the number of concurrent calls you need, on our SIP trunks or yours.
   *Proof needed:* real concurrent-call limits and pricing model.
4. **It's open about being AI.** Callers are told they're speaking to an AI voice agent. That keeps you inside disclosure rules.
   *Proof needed:* the exact disclosure wording the agent uses.

**Angle for each group:**
- **Owners:** "Answer the calls you're missing after 6 pm."
- **Resellers:** "Add AI voice agents to the SIP trunks you already sell."
- **BPO managers:** "Cover call spikes across every client campaign, 24/7."

**Rules for every email** (from `brand/voice.md`):
- Don't promise 100% uptime, guaranteed sales or "callers can't tell it's AI".
- Don't use "revolutionary", "game-changer", "robot", "replace your agents" or "act now".
- Use one calm next step: **Book a demo**.
- Sign off with: Talk soon, The VCAgents Team

## 7. Success metrics

**Main KPI:** Demos booked. Target: **300** by Sun 3 Jan 2027.

**Weekly checkpoints:**

| By end of | Demos booked | Running total |
|---|---|---|
| Week 1 | 120 | 120 |
| Week 2 | 120 | 240 |
| Week 3 | 40 | 280 |
| Week 4 | 20 (plus early-Jan bookings) | 300 |

**Other KPIs:**
- Open rate per email (aim for 40%+ on a warm waitlist)
- Click rate to the booking page (aim for 8%+)
- Booking page conversion (visits to booked demos)
- Demos booked by group (owners, resellers, BPOs)
- Unsubscribes (keep under 0.5% per send)

**How we track:** Email tool reports for opens, clicks and unsubscribes. Booking tool for demos, with a tag showing which email they came from.

**Reporting:** A short weekly check every Friday, plus a full review on Mon 4 Jan.

## 9. Risks and how we handle them

| Risk | Impact | What we do |
|---|---|---|
| **Holiday timing.** Weeks 3 and 4 are Christmas and New Year. Many owners and BPO managers will be away. | Fewer bookings in the second half | Front-load weeks 1–2. Pause sends from 24 Dec to 1 Jan. Offer January demo slots. Or move the whole campaign to start Mon 4 Jan 2027. |
| **The 300 target is high** (15% of the list) | We may fall short | Check after week 1. If under 80 demos, adjust subject lines and offers, and agree a realistic target. |
| **No real proof yet** | Claims sound empty, or break brand rules | Build the proof sheet before writing. Leave out any claim we can't prove. |
| **Waitlist not tagged by group** | Email 3 can't be split | Use Email 1 clicks to sort people. Send the general version to anyone still untagged. |
